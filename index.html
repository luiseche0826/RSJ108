Luis Echevarria's WikiHow Instructions for Broadcast Journalism

An Instruction Challenge for JOUR108

How to make a social media account focused on journalism!

Assembly time: 15 minutes
Awesome rating: 67% awesome

Are you wondering how to make a cool rocket that can easily surpass a baking soda-vinegar rocket? It's really fun to make. Why don't you try to make it yourself? Follow the steps and you'll make a great rocket!

  • Things You'll Need

  • Phone or computer
  • A decent camera
  • Internet access
  • Determination

  1. Define your goals

    When you start your page, it is crucial that you make sure your page firstly appears professional. That means no silly photos of your pets looking silly, no memes or works by other people (without credit that is), and ESPECIALLY no photos of you going out partying!

  2. Focus on a topic

    People follow social media pages because they usually provide a topic that they like. Unfortunately most follows are for the content rather than the person behind, which means if you randomly post yourself dancing to your page that’s full of inspirational quotes, people may be inclined to avoid your content since it is not what they expected it to be. Try to keep your content focused on a topic or multiple, like a page focused on graphic designs you make, or a page where you post your interviews for articles. If you still want to post unprofessional pictures or videos, try to make another account where you know you will not contain any professional information.

    An example of what people's desk setup may look like.

  3. Keep your posts consistant

    People love accounts that consistently post, whether it's a weekly pole about fun facts or a daily photo that you explain the meaning of in the caption. Whenever you decide to post, make sure you try to consistently post again, even if it's two posts a month, many people would find that more engaging than twenty posts in one month and two the next.

  4. Put all your effort and always set new goals

    It shouldn’t need to be said that you should do this for your career, but also for passion. If you hate running your social media account or can’t be bothered to do it, that lack of effort will be reflected in your work. To keep a consistent effort, set goals for yourself, not just one. If you set a single goal and reach it, you’ll likely be confused as to what you should do next since you only set a single goal. Set multiple goals like: having a follower account, getting a collaboration with another journalist, deepening your understanding in the field, etc. When you set multiple goals, you’ll find that you have to keep a constant effort in order to reach all of them and you’ll have enough time to plan more goals once you finish one or two of these.

  5. Focus on being unique

    You should try to separate yourself from that crowd, to stand out and be the one who gets noticed above all the other pages since there are thousands of others who want to do the same thing. Consider creative ideas like a unique constraint or quirk of your posts, like photographing on film and not digitally. Quirks like that draw people to your page as well as leave the impression that you’re putting an effort to stand out. Consistently doing these can also grow your following for that uniqueness. Try to implement new ideas as you go, possibly making new ideas as to what your constraint or quirk will be. One month you can post about local dogs in need, the next you can post about local cats in need. Of course, it shouldn’t be that simple but the focus should stay the same while being unique.
